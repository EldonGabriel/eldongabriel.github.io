<p style="font-size:1.2em; line-height:1.6;">
  This certification focuses on practical IT operations, infrastructure support, system troubleshooting, and operating system administration across Windows and Linux environments.
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.2em; line-height:1.6;">
  Training emphasizes structured troubleshooting methodologies, endpoint support workflows, network fundamentals, and foundational infrastructure management used within operational business environments.
</p>

<p style="text-align:center; font-size: 1.1em; color: #fff; background: rgba(255,255,255,0.05); padding: 15px; border-radius: 8px;">
  <strong>Operational Capabilities:</strong> System Troubleshooting · OS Administration · Infrastructure Support
</p>

<h4>Applied Capability</h4>
<ul>
  <li>Diagnosed hardware, software, and network-related issues using structured troubleshooting workflows</li>
  <li>Administered Windows and Linux systems including processes, permissions, users, and file systems</li>
  <li>Supported foundational infrastructure services including DNS, DHCP, and directory-based environments</li>
  <li>Applied baseline security, maintenance, and operational support practices across endpoint and infrastructure systems</li>
</ul>

<h4>Operational Relevance</h4>
<p>
This certification supports day-to-day IT operations involving endpoint support, infrastructure troubleshooting, system administration, and operational maintenance workflows. Skills developed through this training reinforce technical problem-solving, user support capabilities, and stable infrastructure management within business environments.
</p>
